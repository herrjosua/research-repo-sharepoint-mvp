## Finding
RoomBook's room list shows only each room's name and floor. It does not show how many seats a room
has or whether it has a screen or camera. 4 of 6 people interviewed (P02, P04, P05, P06) said this
makes booking harder, and two had booked a room that was too small.

## What people said
- P06, new hire: "I didn't know how many people it fit."
- P02, project manager: booked a 6-seat room for a 9-person workshop.
- P04, sales representative: does not know which rooms have screens or cameras and has asked the
  office manager in Teams more than once.

## How people work around it
- **A hand-kept cheat sheet.** P01, the office manager, keeps a pinned Teams message listing each
  room's seats and screen, updated "when someone complains". P02 uses it.
- **Asking a person.** P04 asks P01 in Teams. P06 asked a colleague.
- **Personal notes.** P05 keeps her own list of which rooms suit which executive.

## Evidence
N-01 interview notes: P02, P04, P05, P06. P01 is the source of the cheat sheet. P03 uses the same
two rooms every time and did not raise it.

## Limits
Six interviews at one site. The two too-small bookings were reported by participants, not checked
against RoomBook records.

## Related
R-01 readout (idea: show seats and equipment in the room list).
