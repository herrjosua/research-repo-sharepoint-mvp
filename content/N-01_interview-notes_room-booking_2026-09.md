## P01 · Office manager · 2026-09-15
On site five days a week. Can cancel any booking.

- "I get a message a day: it says booked, it's empty, can you cancel it? I can cancel anything. I
  just never know if I should."
- Walks both floors mid-morning and cancels bookings that look abandoned. Says it is a guess.
- Keeps a pinned message in a Teams chat listing each room's seats and screen. "I update it when
  someone complains."
- Weekly staff meeting is a recurring booking. When a week is skipped, the room stays held: "If you
  cancel one week you cancel them all, so nobody does."
- Says "everyone" books rooms they might not need.

## P02 · Project manager · 2026-09-15
On site four days a week.

- "If I peek in and it's empty at five past, I take it."
- Booked a 6-seat room for a 9-person workshop because the list gave no sizes. Now uses P01's Teams
  cheat sheet.
- Books in RoomBook, then types the room name into the Outlook invite. Once forgot, and attendees
  went to the wrong room.
- Booked sprint reviews as a recurring series through year end and does not cancel skipped weeks.
- "I hold the room until I know I don't need it. Letting go is risky."

## P03 · Software engineer · 2026-09-16
On site three days a week.

- "Booked rooms are a suggestion."
- Walks over to look in the window first, then pings the booker in Teams if the room is empty.
- Knows the two rooms he uses, so room details are not a problem for him.
- Pastes the room name into the invite by hand after booking.
- "I book a four-person room as backup. If the good one falls through, I have somewhere."

## P04 · Sales representative · 2026-09-16
In the office two days a week, often with clients.

- "I showed up with a client, the room was booked and empty, so we just sat down. Nobody came."
- Does not know which rooms have screens or cameras and has asked P01 in Teams more than once.
- Avoids RoomBook: messages P01 to book. "Too many steps for two days a week."
- Books late and does not book extra rooms.

## P05 · Executive assistant · 2026-09-17
On site five days a week. Books for three executives.

- Uses the door tablet on floor 3, which shows live occupancy: "The tablet on three shows me if
  anyone's actually in there. It's the only thing I trust."
- Pings the booker in Teams when a floor 2 room looks empty.
- Keeps her own notes on which rooms suit which executive. RoomBook does not show capacity or
  equipment.
- Adds each booking to the executive's calendar by hand.
- Executives' weekly 1:1s are recurring. To skip one date she cancels the whole series and rebooks
  only the dates needed, which takes a long time.
- "If I cancel, I lose it. Someone grabs it in minutes."

## P06 · New hire, three weeks in · 2026-09-17
- Has not yet found a booked room empty.
- Booked a room too small for the team introduction. The list shows room name and floor only: "I
  didn't know how many people it fit."
- Types the room name into the Outlook invite after booking. Was shown how by a colleague.
- Books two rooms for important meetings. "My buddy told me rooms are impossible to get, so I book
  two and cancel one."
