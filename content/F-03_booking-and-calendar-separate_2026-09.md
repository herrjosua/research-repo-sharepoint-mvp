## Finding
RoomBook is separate from the Outlook calendar, so a booking does not create a calendar entry. 4 of
6 people interviewed (P02, P03, P05, P06) book in RoomBook and then add the room to the invite by
hand. A fifth, P04, avoids RoomBook altogether.

## What people said
- P02, project manager: once forgot to add the room name to the invite, and attendees went to the
  wrong room.
- P04, sales representative: "Too many steps for two days a week."

## How people work around it
- **Type the room name into the invite.** P02, P03 and P06 do this after every booking. P06 was
  shown how by a colleague.
- **Update calendars by hand.** P05 adds each booking to the relevant executive's calendar herself.
- **Skip RoomBook.** P04 messages the office manager (P01) in Teams to book for her.

## Evidence
N-01 interview notes: P02, P03, P04, P05, P06.

## Limits
Six interviews at one site. How often the room name is forgotten, and how many wrong-room incidents
result, was not measured.
