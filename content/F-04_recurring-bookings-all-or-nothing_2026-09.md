## Finding
In RoomBook, a recurring booking can be cancelled only as a whole series. 3 of 6 people interviewed
(P01, P02, P05) hold recurring bookings and said that skipping one date means either leaving the
room held or cancelling everything. Most leave it held, which adds to rooms that are booked but
empty (F-01).

## What people said
- P01, office manager: "If you cancel one week you cancel them all, so nobody does."
- P05, executive assistant: cancelling and rebooking only the dates needed "takes a long time".

## How people work around it
- **Leave skipped dates booked.** P01 does this for the weekly staff meeting and P02 for sprint
  reviews booked through year end.
- **Cancel the series and rebook.** P05 does this for executives' weekly 1:1s, rebooking only the
  dates still needed.

## Evidence
N-01 interview notes: P01, P02, P05. The other three participants do not hold recurring bookings.

## Limits
Six interviews at one site. The share of held-but-skipped recurring dates was not measured in the
usage data (A-01).
